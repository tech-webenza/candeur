# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js and React. The deliverable is an interactive, mobile-first wireframe that also preserves its narrative quality on desktop.

## Users

Prospective premium-home buyers arriving mainly from paid media on mobile. They are evaluating a pre-launch residential development in Kukatpally and need enough clarity and trust to register their interest without seeing the final project name or renders.

## Product Purpose

Generate OTP-verified, qualified residential leads while establishing the promise of a nature-led premium 3 BHK development before its official launch.

## Positioning

A 20-acre Candeur development in central Kukatpally with 75% open space, premium 3 BHK residences, and homes planned around openness, air, natural light, and greenery.

## Operating Context

The first release follows the RERA announcement but precedes the formal project launch. Visitors primarily encounter the page on phones after seeing paid advertising. The wireframe will guide subsequent content, SEO, visual design, and development work.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The project name cannot appear before launch.
- Actual project renders, master plan, floor plans, and revealing diagrams cannot appear before launch.
- The signature walkway remains under wraps. Storytelling may suggest uninterrupted walking and nature-led movement only in words, without naming or depicting the feature.
- Describe the offer as 3 BHK residences, 1,552–2,205 sq. ft., at ₹1.3 Cr onwards. Omit “only” and EOI pricing.
- Registration requires mobile OTP verification.
- The enquiry form is not a booking or paid EOI workflow. Do not make payment-free claims. The October sheet separately lists a ₹5 lakh EOI amount. Current OTP is explicitly a demo; production integration remains pending.
- The experience needs one dominant registration action, strong mobile usability, credible project information, and a structure that can accept full project assets after launch.
- Motion must feel premium, remain smooth on mid-range mobile devices, and respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Brand Commitments

- Follow the approved Candeur pre-launch KV language and nature-led positioning.
- Use a premium serif for emotional headlines and a neat sans serif for body copy and controls.
- Use the supplied Kellissa font (requested as Kellisa) for hero and narrative headlines with tight letter spacing, and Archivo for body copy and controls.
- The working thematic territory includes “We made room for you in nature” and the roots, growth, light, and openness story. Final campaign-line wording remains an approval item.
- Candeur remains visible as the developer; the final project name stays hidden until launch.

## Product Principles

- Sell the lived promise without visually pretending to show the finished property.
- Make the mobile experience the primary composition, not a compressed desktop page.
- Pair emotional storytelling with clear price, location, product, and developer proof.
- Show the form upfront for performance traffic; retain a stable form while hero storytelling animates. Follow with amenities, interactive location, plans and residences, developer credentials, and FAQs.
- Use motion to express growth, openness, and continuity while protecting performance and accessibility.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable mobile type, sufficient contrast, visible focus states, touch targets of at least 44 pixels, semantic structure, labeled form controls, clear validation, and a complete reduced-motion mode.
